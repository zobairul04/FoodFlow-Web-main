How FoodFlow Works

🥘

1. Post Surplus Food

Donors list excess food with quantity, expiry time, and pickup address. Our automated safety screener checks the listing.

📍

2. Local Matching & Alerts

Matching verified recipients in the same area or city receive immediate alerts with live countdown timers.

🔑

3. Claim & Pickup Code

Recipients claim portions and receive a unique 8-character code. The donor verifies the code upon handover.
